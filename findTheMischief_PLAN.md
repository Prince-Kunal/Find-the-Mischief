## 1. What we're building

**Find the Mischief** is an offline, single-game Android party app played by passing **one phone** around the group.

- Every player is shown the same secret word (**the Truth**), except **the Trickster(s)**.
- The Trickster may be given a **Whisper** (a hint). Whispers can be turned off, and their strength can be set to **Easy / Medium / Hard**.
- Going around the group, everyone says a one-word clue out loud. Then the group votes to **banish** the player they think is the Trickster.

There is no backend, login, internet permission or ads. The app contains only this one game. A multi-game collection may come later as a **separate app**, so don't build a game hub, registry or "coming soon" screens. Do keep the game logic in its own package so it could be reused later.

## 2. Theme: Norse trickster god

The app's look is inspired by **Loki from Norse mythology**: the god of mischief, shapeshifting and lies, with serpents, runes and a cunning green-and-gold palette.

**IP rule (important):** use only the public-domain mythology. Do **not** use anything from Marvel: no MCU character likeness, no Marvel-style horned helmet design, no TVA or other show references, no movie quotes, no "Marvel" or "MCU" text, no logos. All art must be original (drawn as Compose vectors or generated by us). The app should be safe to publish on the Play Store.

- Palette (put it in `ui/theme/Color.kt`):
  - Background / near-black: `#0B0F0D`
  - Surface / deep emerald: `#0F3D2E`

  - Text on dark / parchment: `#E8DFC8`

- Motifs: Elder Futhark runes (Unicode `ᚠᚢᚦᚨᚱᚲ…`, rendered with **Noto Sans Runic**, OFL) as subtle borders and dividers; a coiled serpent / ouroboros line drawing; gold filigree corners on cards; a faint green smoke or glow effect.
- The reveal card looks like a **rune stone or an old parchment card** with a gold border.
- App icon: an original design, for example a gold serpent coiled into a ring around a green rune on black. Build it as an adaptive icon with a vector foreground.
- Keep the UI readable. Theming must never cost legibility: big words, high contrast, touch targets ≥ 48dp.

### 2.3 Sound and haptics character

The sounds should feel mystical but short: a soft magical shimmer or chime, not a cartoon "boing".

## 5. Game rules and design decisions

### 5.1 Whisper (hint) difficulty

The level describes **how hard the game is for the Trickster**:

| Level | What the Trickster sees | Example (Truth = *Pizza*) |
|---|---|---|
| Easy | A closely related word, so blending in is easy | "Pepperoni" |
| Medium | The category or a broader clue | "Italian food" |
| Hard | A vague, loosely related clue | "Round" |
| Off | No Whisper | — |

Every word in the data file must have all three hints. A hint must **never** contain the secret word or a form of it.

### 5.2 Word data format (`assets/words.json`)

```json
{
  "version": 1,
  "categories": [
    {
      "id": "food",
      "name": "Food",
      "emoji": "🍕",
      "words": [
        { "word": "Pizza", "hints": { "easy": "Pepperoni", "medium": "Italian food", "hard": "Round" } }
      ]
    }
  ]
}
```

Starter categories, each with at least 30 words: Food, Animals, Places, Movies & Shows (generic, e.g. "Superhero movie", not titles), Sports, Objects, Jobs, Bollywood & Cricket (India-flavored, generic terms only, no real people), Festivals, Tech, and a bonus **Norse Myth** category (Thor, Odin, Valhalla, Ragnarok, Yggdrasil… public-domain mythology only).

### 5.3 The pass-the-phone flow (the most important part of the app)

Nobody should ever see another player's card by accident.

1. **Pass screen:** full-screen, dark, with a serpent emblem. It says "Pass the phone to **{name}**" and has a big "I am {name}, reveal my fate" button. Nothing secret is on screen.
2. **Card screen:** shows either the Truth or the Trickster card (§2.1). Use **press-and-hold to reveal**: the card shows only while the finger is down, and a rune stone "glows open". Also offer a "tap to flip" mode in Settings.
3. **"Next player" button:** when pressed, it must
   - play the **pass sound** (a short shimmer or chime),
   - give a **haptic buzz** (works even when the phone is on silent),
   - **immediately** switch to the next player's *Pass screen*, never to their card.

   The sound tells everyone the phone has changed hands, so a player can't quietly peek at the next card.
4. After the last player, show "All fates are sealed." and then start the Telling.

Also:
- **Back button and back gesture are blocked** during reveal, with a confirm dialog: "Abandon the game?"
- Use `FLAG_SECURE` on reveal screens so the card doesn't appear in the Recents thumbnail or screenshots.
- Keep the screen awake while a game is running.
- The card on screen must not flash or animate while it switches, so the old card can't be seen during the transition.

### 5.4 Sound

- `res/raw/pass_chime.ogg`: short (< 0.5 s), mystical, clearly audible. Generate it with a small script (e.g. layered sine tones with a quick shimmer in Python → WAV → OGG via ffmpeg). Commit both the script and the output. Don't download sounds from the internet.
- Also add `reveal_glow`, `judgement_drum` and `timer_end` sounds, made the same way.
- Load everything once with `SoundPool` in `SoundPlayer`. Add a **Sound on/off** setting (default on). The haptic buzz always fires.

### 5.5 Setup options

- Players: 3–20, with add, remove, rename and reorder. Saved names are pre-filled next time.
- Number of Tricksters: 1 up to `max(1, (players - 1) / 3)`.
- Categories: multi-select, default all.
- Whisper: on/off, plus Easy / Medium / Hard.
- Telling timer: off / 30 s / 60 s / 90 s.
- Optional rule: "The Trickster's last lie". If banished, the Trickster can still win by guessing the Truth.

### 5.6 Engine rules (pure Kotlin, in `MischiefEngine`)

- Randomly pick the Trickster(s) and the word from the selected categories.
- Avoid repeating words within a session (keep a used-words set; reset it when the pool runs out).
- Randomly pick the starting player for the Telling, but **never let a Trickster go first**.
- Put the `Random` source behind a parameter so tests are deterministic.

### Phase 5 — Role reveal (pass-the-phone)
- Build §5.3 exactly, plus `SoundPlayer`, `Haptics`, `FLAG_SECURE`, back-blocking and keep-screen-on.
- **Done when:** on my phone, every "Next player" press plays the chime and buzzes, and I can't reach anyone's card without going through their Pass screen.

### Phase 6 — The Telling (clue round)
- Show the starting player and the order (names arranged in a circle, like a ring of runes).
- Optional countdown timer with the `timer_end` sound.
- A "Another round" button and a "Begin the Judgement" button.

### Phase 7 — The Judgement and results
- Voting mode A (default): the group decides out loud and taps the player to banish. Confirm before banishing.
- Voting mode B: secret vote. Pass the phone, each player taps a suspect (pass screen + chime between players), then show the tally.
- Result: dramatic reveal (`judgement_drum` sound) saying "Mischief found!" or "The Trickster prevails.", plus the Truth. If "last lie" is on and the Trickster was banished, show a text field where the Trickster can type a guess (case-insensitive, ignoring surrounding spaces).
- With multiple Tricksters, voting continues until all Tricksters are out or the Tricksters equal the remaining Honest players.
- Buttons: "Play again (same players)" and "Change setup".

### Phase 8 — Polish
- Settings screen: sound, reveal mode (hold vs tap).
- Screen transition animations (green smoke or fade), which must never animate a card into view.
- Original adaptive launcher icon (§2.2) and a themed splash screen (SplashScreen API).
- Accessibility check: content descriptions, large touch targets, readable contrast.

### Phase 9 — Release prep
- Turn on R8/minify for release, generate a signing key (walk me through it, and don't commit the keystore), build a signed AAB and APK.
- Write a short README.
